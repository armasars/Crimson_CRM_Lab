    Roadmap

    From rules to real learning

    A J48 decision tree predicted the purchase category for only 18.5% of customers (Kappa −0.05). Demographics alone are not enough; Crimson needs richer data.

    1. Now

      Segment rules

      Lift-based picks for new customers. Solves cold start.

    2. Now

      Smart coupons + RFM

      Self-learning offers and automatic win-back messages.

    3. Collect next

      Richer data

      Repeat purchases, browsing and search, abandoned carts, order time, household and diet survey.

    4. Then

      Collaborative filtering

      "Customers like you also bought", retrained weekly.
